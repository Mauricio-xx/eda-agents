Part 4 · PDK4 / 5

PDK choice — point at the wafer-space fork

All LibreLane steps in Ep 03 set --pdk-root to a local clone of wafer-space/gf180mcu (tag 1.8.0), not the docker's bundled gf180mcuD. Two reasons.

1 · CHIP-TOP REQUIRES IT

The padring instantiates gf180mcu_ws_io__dvdd / dvss — IO cells the docker's stock PDK does not ship.

2 · DOCKER PDK HAS STALE CONFIG

Its libs.tech/librelane/config.tcl is OpenLane 1.x; LibreLane v3 silently skips KLayout DRC. metrics.csv looks clean, signoff is incomplete.

Upstream fix tracked in RTimothyEdwards/open_pdks#507 — open, unmerged. Until it lands, the fork is the only path to full LibreLane v3 signoff on gf180mcu.
